Software and hardware, designed together

The software

A desktop design app and a monitoring dashboard. You draw your tanks, pumps, and sensors on a screen. MajiFlow checks the design, then generates the controller firmware and dashboard.

The hardware

Standard ESP32 controllers, sensors, pumps, and valves. MajiFlow produces the firmware that connects every part and powers the dashboard.

The two parts meet in the field. Firmware generated by the app gets flashed onto each controller. The controllers read your sensors, switch your pumps and valves, and report back to the dashboard. You own the full stack. No cloud subscriptions, no recurring costs.

Monitor

See tank levels, flow rates, and valve states in one dashboard. Know what is happening even when you are off-site.
