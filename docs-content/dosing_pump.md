This site has **{{node_kind_count}} dosing pump(s)** — a small metering pump that injects a chemical or nutrient additive into the line (chlorination, fertigation, pH correction).

- It is relay- or PWM-driven and meters a controlled dose; it does not move the main flow.
- Dose alongside an active route so the additive mixes into moving water, not a static pipe.
- Keep the additive's wetted parts chemically compatible, and fit a non-return valve so dosed line pressure can't push back into the additive tank.
