The MajiFlow Limited Warranty for {{site_name}}. Your commissioning date and the controllers it covers are listed under **Site details** in your generated site document; the term runs 12 months from that date.

## What's covered

- The firmware MajiFlow generates matches the design you validated.
- The MajiFlow-branded controller(s) are free from defects in materials and workmanship. This site has {{controller_count}} controller(s).

## Term

12 months from activation, measured from the commissioning date (when your first controller went live).

- Commissioned: {{commission_date}}
- Covered until: {{warranty_expiry}}

## How a claim is resolved

At MajiFlow's option, one of:

- Re-generate or correct the firmware.
- Advance-ship a replacement controller for you to fit yourself; your configuration restores automatically on the new unit. A deposit or shipping cost may apply.
- Refund the fee for the affected controller.

On-site service is not included under this warranty, but is available separately at additional cost.

## What keeps it valid

Coverage holds while a controller runs unmodified MajiFlow firmware with its safety interlocks enabled. Because every controller reports its own state, eligibility is confirmed automatically: doing the routine in **Maintenance** leaves the record that keeps your cover in place.

Coverage ends automatically if the telemetry shows safety interlocks were bypassed, firmware was modified outside MajiFlow, or the system was run through standing alarms.

## What's not covered

Installation and wiring, water and agronomic outcomes (crop yield, water quality), third-party hardware, water ingress or physical damage (surge, impact, an unsealed enclosure), and anything beyond what MajiFlow supplied.

## Limits

MajiFlow is not liable for indirect or consequential loss (crop loss, water damage, livestock, property, or business loss). Total liability is capped at the amount you paid MajiFlow. Safety interlocks run on the controller itself and fail safe: a lost connection never disables them.

## Support and Enterprise

Day-to-day support is best-effort; this warranty is not a support or uptime contract. Custom and Enterprise installations may add a service-level agreement, support, and on-site service under a separate agreement that supplements this warranty and overrides it where they conflict.
