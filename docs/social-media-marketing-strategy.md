# MajiFlow Social Media Strategy

**Team:** 1 person.  
**Time budget:** ~10 hours/week.  
**Partner:** Plum — irrigation, agrovet & pharmacy partner in Kenya (Nairobi, Matuu).  
**Anchor:** MajiFlow is the **local-first** water controller — it keeps your pump safe even when the internet dies.  
**Beachhead:** Kenyan irrigation farmers and greenhouse operators.

The homepage already carries the right bones: Plum as the trusted supply partner, the “Built to keep going” resilience band, and the “runs without internet or subscription” proof point. Social media’s job is to **sharpen and amplify** what is already there, not invent a new story.

---

## 1. What the Homepage Tells Us

| Asset | State | Social use |
|---|---|---|
| **Hero** | “Watch and control your water, from anywhere” | Lead message works, but offline safety is stronger for this market. Test both. |
| **Plum partner block** | Live, with logo, locations, contact | Use as credibility in every ad and post. |
| **Verticals** | Farms, hotels, greenhouses, remote sites | Lead with **Farms**. Others are proof of range. |
| **Resilience band** | “Runs on its own, subscription or not” | This is your sharpest hook. Make it the star. |
| **How it works** | Cinematic simulation page | Link from LinkedIn/YouTube; use screenshots in carousels. |
| **Pricing** | Assessment-based, no public sticker | Drive social traffic here for qualified leads. |
| **Testimonial** | Commented out | Biggest single content gap. Fix this first. |

---

## 2. The Sharpened Anchor

The homepage already says the right thing in the resilience band. Social media should lead with it:

> **“Your pump keeps running even when the internet dies.”**

The split MajiFlow owns:

| Cloud controller | MajiFlow |
|---|---|
| Brain in the app/cloud | Brain on the controller |
| Needs an active connection for cloud automations | Safety automations run locally on the controller |
| Remote until it isn't | Remote + autonomous |

**Word to own:** **local** / **works offline**.  
**Enemy:** the assumption that smart water control needs a live connection.

---

## 3. Biggest Levers for One Person

1. **Get one real testimonial.** The homepage has a commented-out slot. A quote from a Plum customer or early site is the highest-impact content you can add.
2. **Film the offline proof video.** Show the controller running with WiFi disabled. Use Plum’s location or a real site if possible.
3. **Verify tracking.** Check if Meta Pixel and pricing-page events are installed. If not, install them this week.
4. **Set up WhatsApp Business.** This is where East African leads convert.
5. **Repurpose homepage assets.** Screenshot the resilience band, the Plum block, and the “how it works” simulation.
6. **Founder-led LinkedIn.** 3 posts/week. Lead with offline safety and the Plum partnership.
7. **Facebook groups.** Kenyan farming/irrigation groups. 30 min/day answering questions.
8. **One small ad test.** KES 500/day for 5 days to `/pricing`, creative = offline proof video.

## 5. Content Factory (1 → 5)

One core asset per week, sliced across channels.

| Core asset | Slices |
|---|---|
| Plum customer testimonial (60 sec) | LinkedIn post, Facebook video, WhatsApp status, ad creative, homepage testimonial slot |
| Offline proof video | YouTube short, LinkedIn video, Facebook video, WhatsApp status, ad |
| Screenshot from “how it works” | LinkedIn carousel, Instagram, Facebook, blog embed |
| Founder lesson learned | Twitter thread, LinkedIn post, Reddit comment, Facebook text |

**Rule:** every slice must mention or imply “works offline / local.”

---

## 6. Post Formulas

### LinkedIn
- Hook: cloud problem or Plum story.
- Body: cost of failure.
- Proof: one metric, quote, or screenshot.
- CTA: link in comments to `/pricing` or WhatsApp.

Example hook: *“Plum sees farms every week whose smart pump stopped working the moment the internet dropped. MajiFlow was built so that never happens.”*

### Facebook
- Native video or photo from a farm/site.
- Plain language, Swahili/English if natural.
- CTA: DM for quote or visit `/pricing`.

### WhatsApp Status
- 15–30 sec clips.
- Text overlay: “Internet down. Pump protected.”

---

## 7. Paid Social (Minimal)

Start with KES 500/day on Facebook. Creative options:

- Offline proof video.
- Plum partnership graphic: “Available at Plum branches in Nairobi and Matuu.”
- Screenshot of the “Built to keep going” band.

Target: Kenya, interests = farming, irrigation, agriculture, solar water pump, greenhouse.

Objective: traffic to `/pricing`.

Scale only when cost per qualified lead is clear.

## 8. 30-Day Sprint

| Week | Focus | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Fill the gaps | Get testimonial permission/quote. Verify Pixel. Film offline proof video. Set up WhatsApp Business. |
| **2** | Launch content | 3 LinkedIn posts using Plum story. Daily Facebook group comments. Run KES 500/day ad. |
| **3** | Repurpose | Slice testimonial into 5 pieces. Post YouTube short. Use homepage “how it works” screenshots. |
| **4** | Optimize | Check cheapest qualified lead by channel. Double down. Kill losers. |

## 9. Metrics

| Metric | Target / note |
|---|---|

| WhatsApp inquiries | 5–10/week |

| Facebook posting + group comments | 2 hrs |

---

## 11. What to Ignore

- TikTok, podcasts, influencer agencies, press releases.
- Verticals outside farms/irrigation as primary content.
- Any content that does not reinforce “works offline” or the Plum proof.

---

## Bottom Line

The homepage already has the right story: Plum as the trusted partner, and MajiFlow as the system that keeps running. Social media should not create a new narrative — it should **make the existing one unavoidable** in Kenyan farming feeds.

Do that for 90 days before adding anything else.
